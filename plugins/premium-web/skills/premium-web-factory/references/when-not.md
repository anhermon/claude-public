# When not to use the factory

- **The page is outside morph-subject or rail.** Multi-object scenes, a scroll-scrubbed video, bespoke pinned storytelling: use
  premium-web-webgl or premium-web-motion directly. Code-only panel rails are supported (`grammars.md`).
- **The brief demands bespoke art direction in every section.** The factory offers two grammars; it will give a baseline, not
  a distinctive page. Use it for the baseline and hand-build what is distinctive, or skip it.
- **A real product with real data.** Factory pieces are concept pages: invented names, labelled numbers. Do not point one at a real
  brand without rewriting the copy and the notice.
- **Dashboards, forms, app UI, docs sites.** Wrong grammar entirely; see premium-web `references/forms.md` and the relevant UI skill.
- **Characters, mascots, hand-modelled objects.** animated-objects skills.
- **You need images.** The factory stores none. Ask premium-web-imagery for plates and wire them through the asset cache.
- **One-off page you will never regenerate.** The factory pays off when a spec is edited and rebuilt several times, or when several
  pieces share a grammar. For a single throwaway, a hand-built page may be as fast.

What it is good at: a credible, fully working baseline in seconds so the review time goes to the wow moment and the copy.
